🏛️ Sister Concern #1 • Architectural & Engineering Division

Touch Point Associates

Turnkey Commercial Architecture, Banking Fit-outs & Modular Workstation Engineering

Touch Point Associates is our specialized corporate interior architecture and civil engineering division. Led by registered architects and seasoned civil engineers, the firm provides end-to-end turnkey architectural design, space planning, modular workstations, and construction documentation for leading banks, corporate headquarters, and high-spec institutions across Bangladesh.

With our dedicated manufacturing plant in Satarkul, Badda, and dedicated design studio in Gulshan-2, Touch Point Associates fabricates customized commercial fixtures with precision German machinery, flame-retardant melamine panels, and heavy-duty steel frameworks.
