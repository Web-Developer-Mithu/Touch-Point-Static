Built By Master Craftsmen in Dhaka

At Touch Point, every dining table, bed, and executive workstation is crafted with utmost perfection across our Satarkul and Uttar Badda manufacturing plants and Banasree flagship studio. We combine computerized CNC precision joinery with traditional hand-carving techniques honed over two decades of industrial experience.
