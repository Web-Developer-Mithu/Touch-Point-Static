Company Overview

One of the Leading Interior & Furniture Conglomerates in Bangladesh

With an average annual growth of over 50%, TOUCH POINT is at present one of the most accomplished and vertically integrated business houses in Bangladesh. Bonded with three thriving business units, Touch Point stands as a living testament to purposeful entrepreneurship and quality control.

Touch Point owns and successfully operates modern manufacturing and trading concerns led by the prudent leadership of its founder & proprietor Mr. Md. Ariful Islam, along with distinguished architects, civil engineers, and chartered financial specialists.

Secondly, our setup of modern factory facilities in Satarkul and Uttar Badda ventures into every facet of the lifestyles of Bangladeshis. Today, Touch Point dominates the consumer and corporate market for seasoned timber, melamine coated particleboard, modular workstations, and ergonomic executive seating with eco-friendly and flame-retardant standards.

Analogous high-spec raw materials, precision CNC cutting, in-house seasoning kilns, and centralized logistics enable Touch Point to deliver bespoke quality at unmatchable factory-direct efficiency.
